# Rockbot

> Rockbot is a unified in-location media platform for businesses — background music, audio messaging, digital signage, Rockbot TV, music videos, and retail-media advertising, controlled across many venues from one system. The Rockbot v5 REST API lets customers programmatically control music playback and playlist overrides per zone, run audio-messaging and signage campaigns by group or zone, upload and attach assets, check device status/screenshots and reboot players, and pull or export playback history. Auth is OAuth 2.0 client-credentials (24-hour bearer tokens); default rate limit 1 request/second.

## Auth

- OAuth 2.0 Client Credentials grant. Email support@rockbot.com to request a CLIENT_ID and one-time CLIENT_SECRET.
- Token endpoint: POST https://api.rockbot.com/v5/api-clients/token with {client_id, client_secret}
- Send the returned token as `Authorization: Bearer ACCESS_TOKEN`. Tokens are valid 24 hours.
