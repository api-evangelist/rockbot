## Key Differentiators
- Only platform combining music, digital signage, TV, and retail media natively — one contract, one dashboard, one support team
- Music covers ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, and GMR — fully licensed for commercial business use
- Official Amazon Signage Stick launch partner — deepest hardware integration in the category
- Spotify playlist import for business (licensed for commercial use)
- Franchise-grade controls: brand-level standards with per-location flexibility
- Retail media network powered by Amazon In-Store Marketplace

## Products
- [Music](https://rockbot.com/music-for-business): Fully licensed background music streaming for businesses. Covers ASCAP, BMI, SESAC, and GMR. Millions of songs, curated playlists, Spotify import, smart scheduling, and multi-location control.
- [Digital Signage](https://rockbot.com/digital-signage-for-business): Cloud CMS for screens across unlimited locations. Works with Amazon Signage Stick, Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Android, and more. Real-time updates, dynamic content, franchise brand controls.
- [TV for Business](https://rockbot.com/tv-for-business): Streaming TV with audio-optional channels, remote scheduling, and competitor ad blocking. No cable contract required.
- [Retail Media](https://rockbot.com/retail-media): Monetize in-store screens and audio with ads via Amazon In-Store Marketplace. Available to businesses of any size — not just enterprise retailers.
- [Audio Messaging](https://rockbot.com/audio-messaging): Scheduled promotional and informational announcements integrated into the music experience.
- [Rockbot Request](https://rockbot.com/request): Guest song request feature that drives customer engagement in-venue.
- [Advertising](https://rockbot.com/advertising): Reach consumers in high-traffic physical locations through Rockbot's ad network.
